Report 01 · what the evidence supports

Why 40% of AI agents will fail — and the four-tier fix.

Gartner expects four in ten enterprises to demote or decommission autonomous AI agents by 2027. The cause is not the agents. It is treating governance as a switch — locked down or fully trusted — instead of a dial.

The short · 1:42

Binary governance is the failure mode. A four-level autonomy taxonomy (Observe, Advise, Act with approval, Act autonomously) lets controls scale with what an agent can actually do.

Evidence grades
Traced
40% of enterprises will demote or decommission AI agents by 2027.Gartner, Inc. announcement, 26 May 2026. A forecast, not a measurement: it predicts behaviour, so it should be read as an analyst projection.
Attributed
Binary governance is the root cause of agent failure.Quoted from Shiva Varma, Senior Director Analyst at Gartner, in the same announcement. An expert view supported by the two failure modes it describes.
Framework
Four autonomy levels: Observe, Advise, Act with approval, Act autonomously.A classification, not a finding. Its value is that controls can be attached to each level and stack as autonomy increases.
Illustrative
A circuit breaker should trip at a 2% error rate.The 2% is a worked example in the report, not a tested threshold. Set the real number from your own baseline error rate.
The next move

Pick one AI agent or automation already running in your business. Write down which of the four levels it sits at, what it can read, what it can write, and who owns it. If any of those four answers takes more than a minute, that is the governance gap, and it is where the first control goes.
